Health & safety guide

Simple, practical reminders for common travel situations. For urgent or serious symptoms, seek professional help.

☀

Heat and heatwaves

  • Drink water regularly and rest in shade or a cool place.
  • Avoid strenuous activity during the hottest part of the day.
  • Seek urgent medical help for confusion, fainting or severe overheating.

Flooding and heavy rain

  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Move away from fast-flowing water and low-lying areas.
  • Follow official evacuation and road-closure instructions.

Air pollution

  • Check local air-quality advisories.
  • Reduce strenuous outdoor activity when air quality is poor.
  • People with existing heart or lung conditions should follow their clinician's advice.

Travel health

  • Carry essential medicines and a small first-aid kit.
  • Wash hands and use safe drinking water.
  • Keep allergies and important medical information accessible.
