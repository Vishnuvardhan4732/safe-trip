What to do in an emergency

1

Move to safety

Stay away from immediate danger where possible. Do not enter floodwater or unsafe buildings.

2

Call for help

Contact the local emergency number. Give your location, what happened and how many people need help.

3

Follow instructions

Follow emergency operator and local authority guidance. Do not delay professional help to use this website.

4

Tell someone you trust

Share your location and travel plan with a trusted person when safe to do so.

Keep these details ready

  • Your exact location or nearest landmark
  • Emergency contact and accommodation details
  • Relevant allergies or essential medical information
  • Phone battery and an alternative way to contact help
SafeTrip does not place calls or dispatch services. Use your phone to contact emergency services directly. Emergency numbers and availability vary by country.
